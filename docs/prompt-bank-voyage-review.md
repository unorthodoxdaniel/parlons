# Voyage: shorter questions for review

30 approved English questions, including the revised TR26. Imported in English and French on 24 October 2026 through migration 20261024120000_add_voyage_prompts.sql. Existing questions preserved. Canonical bilingual source: prompt-bank-voyage.csv. IDs continue after TR20.

Editorial direction: stay with Voyage for the next batches. Aim for roughly two displayed lines, with three reserved for an unusually strong idea. Actual wrapping depends on screen width and language, so favour one short setup and one central question over multiple instructions. Let people find their own reasons without always adding “explain why.”

## Comfort, company and money

- **TR21.** A five-star hotel alone or a hostel with your best friends: which feels more like a holiday?
- **TR22.** Same price, same destination: a private hotel room or a hostel where you might make friends?
- **TR23.** Would you rather travel cheaply every year or luxuriously once every five years?
- **TR24.** Your friends choose a trip you can barely afford. Do you ask them to change it or sit this one out?
- **TR25.** What would you happily save on during a trip, and what would ruin it if you went cheap?
- **TR26.** Your friend pays for your holiday, then expects you to follow their itinerary every day. Can you say no?

## Who you travel with

- **TR27.** Can someone be a great friend and a terrible travel companion?
- **TR28.** On a group holiday, is spending a whole day alone rude or necessary?
- **TR29.** You paid extra for comfort; your friend chose the cheapest option. Should you split up for the journey?
- **TR30.** Is a holiday together a fair test of a relationship?
- **TR31.** One friend takes all the photos but appears in none. Whose job is it to notice?
- **TR32.** One person plans the whole trip. Do they get the final say when everyone else complains?

## What makes a trip worthwhile

- **TR33.** Is doing nothing on holiday a waste of the trip or the whole point?
- **TR34.** Would you cross the world for one concert, meal or football match?
- **TR35.** Can a place disappoint you and still be worth visiting?
- **TR36.** Would you rather miss the famous sights or miss the chance to wander?
- **TR37.** What's an ordinary moment from a trip that you'd choose to live again?
- **TR38.** When does adapting to local customs start to feel like pretending to be someone else?
- **TR39.** Can you love travelling and still dislike being a tourist?
- **TR40.** Is it selfish to keep a beautiful place secret?

## Social media, technology and public debate

These are topical conversation angles, not claims about what is trending this week. Use specific news or viral events only after checking the context. A prompt should still make sense without having seen the original post.

- **TR41.** Your dream destination goes viral. Does that make you more excited to visit or less?
- **TR42.** Should influencers ask permission before sharing the location of a quiet local spot?
- **TR43.** If a place looks better in photos than in person, who let you down: the place or the posts?
- **TR44.** Would you trust an AI to plan a surprise holiday if you couldn't change the itinerary?
- **TR45.** If translation apps worked perfectly, would learning a few local phrases still matter?
- **TR46.** A café bans photos. Does that make you want to visit or go somewhere else?
- **TR47.** Should visitors pay to enter a city that residents can barely afford to live in?
- **TR48.** Would you take an overnight train instead of a short flight if it cost twice as much?
- **TR49.** Does visiting a cheaper version of your dream destination feel clever or disappointing?
- **TR50.** When does filming your holiday start to spoil it for the people travelling with you?

## Suggested first picks

TR21, TR24, TR27, TR28, TR33, TR40, TR42 and TR50. They offer an immediate position to take, with room for personal stories and disagreement. TR21 explores comfort versus familiar company; TR22 separately explores privacy versus meeting new people. They are alternative treatments of the hotel/hostel idea and need not both be selected.
