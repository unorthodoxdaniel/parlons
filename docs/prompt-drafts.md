# Prompt taste test: 20 originals

English review drafts, not imported. Select keep / boring / more like this before
expanding or adapting naturally into French. These explore taste rather than
claiming to reflect the creator's preferences.

1. What harmless thing do you judge people for? Defend your unreasonable opinion.
2. Which ordinary experience deserves the kind of review people give films or restaurants?
3. You can remove one unwritten social rule. Which one goes, and what happens next?
4. Tell us about a time you changed your mind but didn't want to admit it.
5. Which small luxury makes your life noticeably better? Convince someone who thinks it's a waste.
6. Would you rather be understood by a few people or admired by many? What would you give up?
7. Describe a place that feels like home even though you've never lived there.
8. Is there something we call convenient that actually makes life worse?
9. You must teach a class on something you know from experience, without researching it. What do you teach?
10. Which fictional world would be wonderful to visit but terrible to live in?
11. Tell us about a conversation you still replay in your head. What would you say differently now?
12. Which activity should people be allowed to be bad at without trying to improve?
13. You have to spend a month living by a friend's habits. Whom do you choose, and what would be hardest?
14. When does being honest become being unkind? Invent a situation where the line is difficult to draw.
15. What do people misunderstand about a place or community you know well?
16. Would a four-day workweek change how you live, or would the extra day fill up anyway?
17. What is something you enjoy that you struggle to explain to other people?
18. Your neighbourhood gets a small budget to make everyday life better. What should it spend it on?
19. Is boredom something we should escape, or something we need more of?
20. Pick an object you own. Tell its story as though it belongs in a museum.
