# Prompt bank expansion: 100 review drafts

> Historical draft batch. Final selection, approved rewrites and French adaptations were imported on 21 October 2026; see [the import record](prompt-bank-expansion.md). The original draft wording below is preserved for reference.

Status: English review drafts only. Not imported or approved. Ten new ideas per existing theme. French adaptations follow editorial review, so rejected ideas do not get translated unnecessarily. Approving all drafts would add 200 prompt rows after adaptation, bringing the current 306-row bank to 506.

## Review

Use the stable IDs to reply with **keep**, **cut**, **rewrite**, or **more like this**. Unmarked prompts remain unreviewed. Invented stories are always welcome; personal experience and private disclosure are never required. Prompts should work without specialist knowledge or research.

## Everyday life (`everyday-life`)

- **EL01.** Which cheap object would you be most annoyed to lose? Explain why replacing it would not be quite the same.
- **EL02.** You can make one everyday item twice as big or half its size. What do you change, and who benefits?
- **EL03.** Give a passionate defence of a food combination other people find strange.
- **EL04.** Two people share a kitchen: one cleans while cooking, the other cleans afterward. Negotiate an arrangement they can both live with.
- **EL05.** Which everyday task looks easy until you have to explain every step to a beginner?
- **EL06.** Your electricity will be off for an evening, with enough notice to prepare. Plan an evening you might actually enjoy.
- **EL07.** A visitor can observe your home but cannot ask questions. What might they wrongly conclude about you?
- **EL08.** You find a shopping list with five very different items. Invent the story behind it.
- **EL09.** Which item do people buy for the person they hope to become? Explain what happens when it gets home.
- **EL10.** You are leaving instructions for someone looking after your home. What detail would be obvious to you but surprising to them?

## Relationships (`relationships`)

- **RE01.** A friend asks for your opinion but seems to want encouragement. How would you work out what they need?
- **RE02.** Two friends remember the same event very differently. Does either of them need to win the argument?
- **RE03.** You receive a generous invitation to something you would hate. Decline it without making the host regret asking.
- **RE04.** Someone keeps giving you advice you did not request. What could you say while keeping the relationship warm?
- **RE05.** Can a friendship work when one person loves debating and the other finds disagreement exhausting?
- **RE06.** You are introducing two friends who have almost nothing in common. How would you get their conversation started?
- **RE07.** What kind of compliment is hardest to forget? Give a real or invented example.
- **RE08.** A group chat has gone quiet. Should someone revive it, or can it end naturally?
- **RE09.** You lend something to a friend and it comes back damaged. What matters most in the conversation that follows?
- **RE10.** Is it better to ask someone how they want to be supported or to take the initiative? Describe when each approach helps.

## Culture (`culture`)

- **CU01.** A film you disliked keeps coming up in conversation. Explain what it did well before explaining why it did not work for you.
- **CU02.** You can hear one song again as though you have never heard it before. Which would you choose, and where would you listen?
- **CU03.** Does knowing how much effort went into something change how much you enjoy it?
- **CU04.** Tell a familiar story from the point of view of a character who usually gets ignored.
- **CU05.** A restaurant removes your favourite dish because almost nobody orders it. Make the case for keeping it—or explain why you would accept the decision.
- **CU06.** What makes something feel dated in a charming way rather than an embarrassing way?
- **CU07.** You must recommend a work you have never finished. Explain why it still deserves someone's attention.
- **CU08.** Would you rather know the ending of a story in advance or know nothing at all? Explain what you gain and lose.
- **CU09.** A local cinema can show only one film this month. How should it choose something for a community with different tastes?
- **CU10.** Choose a minor character from a story you know. What would their ordinary day look like after the story ends?

## Society (`society`)

- **SO01.** A busy park has space for either a quiet garden or a sports court. Present the strongest case for each before choosing.
- **SO02.** Should a popular free event require reservations if many people reserve and never arrive?
- **SO03.** A town wants to rename a street. Who should have a say, and what would make the process feel fair?
- **SO04.** What should people be allowed to do in a public square without buying anything?
- **SO05.** A neighbourhood noticeboard is full of complaints. Write the first positive notice that might change its tone.
- **SO06.** Should a shop let people use its toilet without making a purchase? Consider the situation from both sides.
- **SO07.** A community garden produces more vegetables than its volunteers can use. Decide how the surplus should be shared.
- **SO08.** What would you put in a welcome pack for someone who has just moved to your town, beyond maps and advertisements?
- **SO09.** Is a small courtesy still valuable when everyone is expected to perform it?
- **SO10.** A street is closed to cars for one day. Describe what would convince residents to try it again—or decide against it.

## Memories (`memories`)

- **ME01.** Tell the story of an ordinary day that became memorable for a very small reason. You may invent it.
- **ME02.** What did you misunderstand as a child that made perfect sense to you at the time?
- **ME03.** Describe a place you remember mainly through the route you took to get there.
- **ME04.** Think of an old photograph, real or imagined. What happened just outside the frame?
- **ME05.** Tell a story about waiting for something. Was the anticipation better or worse than the event?
- **ME06.** Describe something you used to find difficult that you now do without thinking.
- **ME07.** Which small detail would help you recreate a room from your past?
- **ME08.** Tell the story of an accidental discovery: a place, a recipe, a song or something else.
- **ME09.** Imagine finding a notebook you kept ten years ago. What would you hope to find in it, and what might puzzle you?
- **ME10.** Describe a moment when you realised you had become familiar with a place that once felt unfamiliar.

## Learning (`learning`)

- **LE01.** Teach someone a simple game without showing them the rules. How would you check they understood?
- **LE02.** Is it easier to learn from someone who is naturally talented or someone who struggled? Explain your choice.
- **LE03.** You have learnt a new word but keep forgetting to use it. Invent a situation that would make it stick.
- **LE04.** Describe something you can recognise when it is done well but cannot do well yourself.
- **LE05.** A beginner asks a question that everyone else is afraid to ask. How should the teacher respond?
- **LE06.** You must choose between clear feedback that is uncomfortable and encouraging feedback that is vague. What would you ask for?
- **LE07.** When does copying an example help you learn, and when does it stop you thinking?
- **LE08.** Explain a concept using an analogy, then point out where your analogy breaks down.
- **LE09.** You discover that a method you were taught is unnecessarily complicated. Would you keep it, adapt it or start again?
- **LE10.** How would you tell whether you are improving at something that has no scores or exams?

## Imagination (`imagination`)

- **IM01.** A lost-property office receives things people have forgotten emotionally: courage, patience, curiosity. Describe its busiest day.
- **IM02.** Every building must have one room with no practical purpose. Design yours.
- **IM03.** For one day, everyone can hear the background music that matches your mood. How would you spend the day?
- **IM04.** You inherit a shop that sells only things nobody needs anymore. What is its most surprising bestseller?
- **IM05.** A door in your home opens to a different place each Sunday. What rules would you make before stepping through?
- **IM06.** People can exchange talents for a week, but neither person can use their own talent during the swap. Propose an exchange.
- **IM07.** The moon is available for one short advertisement. What message would you put there, if any?
- **IM08.** A tiny dragon has moved into a public library. Explain how the librarian persuades everyone it can stay.
- **IM09.** You receive a postcard from a city that does not exist. Describe the message and what you do next.
- **IM10.** Invent a competition in which finishing first is a disadvantage. Explain how someone wins.

## Work (`work`)

- **WO01.** A meeting ends with everyone agreeing, but nobody knows what to do next. How would you rescue it?
- **WO02.** You have a useful idea that would create extra work for someone else. How would you propose it fairly?
- **WO03.** A new colleague asks why a task is done a certain way, and nobody knows. What should happen next?
- **WO04.** Which matters more on someone's first day: feeling useful or feeling welcome? Design a first day around your answer.
- **WO05.** Two colleagues do equally valuable work, but only one is visible to the rest of the team. How could their contributions be recognised fairly?
- **WO06.** You finish a task much earlier than expected. What would you do with the remaining time, and what might influence your decision?
- **WO07.** A team has too many priorities. How would you help it decide what it will stop doing?
- **WO08.** Describe a workplace rule that sounds sensible but could have an unexpected downside.
- **WO09.** You must explain a delay without blaming anyone or pretending everything is fine. What would you say?
- **WO10.** Would you rather have a predictable working day or a different challenge every day? Explain when your preference might change.

## Travel (`travel`)

- **TR01.** You have promised to bring home a souvenir but have very little luggage space. What would you look for?
- **TR02.** A visitor has only one hour in your area. What would you show them, and what would you deliberately leave out?
- **TR03.** You and a travel companion want completely different kinds of holiday. Plan one day that gives each of you something to enjoy.
- **TR04.** You arrive somewhere and discover you have misunderstood a local custom. How would you recover gracefully?
- **TR05.** Would you rather stay in one place for a month or visit four places in the same time? Explain what you would miss either way.
- **TR06.** Describe the first thing you notice when returning home after time away.
- **TR07.** A guidebook describes a place you know well. What might it miss even if every fact is correct?
- **TR08.** You must choose a destination based on one question you can ask someone who lives there. What would you ask?
- **TR09.** Tell the story of a journey from the point of view of a suitcase.
- **TR10.** You can take a visitor to one ordinary place that tourists rarely see. Explain why it deserves their time.

## Technology (`technology`)

- **TE01.** A messaging app adds a button that explains why someone has not replied. Would you use it? What could go wrong?
- **TE02.** Which device feature would you find hardest to explain to someone from fifty years ago?
- **TE03.** An app keeps changing where its buttons are. Explain when a redesign is worth making users learn again.
- **TE04.** Your photo library has thousands of pictures. How would you choose ten that are worth keeping?
- **TE05.** Would you rather own a simpler device outright or rent a better one that depends on a subscription?
- **TE06.** A navigation app finds a shortcut through a quiet residential street. Whose interests should it consider?
- **TE07.** You can add a confirmation step to one online action and remove it from another. Which actions do you choose?
- **TE08.** A device works perfectly but no longer receives updates. What would you want to know before deciding whether to keep it?
- **TE09.** Explain how you would help someone recover from sending a message to the wrong group chat.
- **TE10.** Imagine a search engine that gives you one good question before giving you an answer. When would that be useful or irritating?
